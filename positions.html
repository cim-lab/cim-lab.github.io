Funded Ph.D. Position — AI-Enabled Wireless & Autonomous Systems

Collective Intelligence in Motion (CIM) Lab · Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi · Starting Spring 2027

Open

We are recruiting a highly motivated Ph.D. student to join the lab starting in Spring 2027. The student will work on AI-enabled wireless and autonomous systems, including emerging directions such as wave-domain computing and Stacked Intelligent Metasurfaces (SIM).

Core Research Areas

  • UAV-assisted communications & connected/autonomous vehicles
  • Intelligent metasurfaces (RIS/SIM)
  • Edge computing & wireless resource allocation
  • Optimization & reinforcement learning

Ideal Candidate

  • B.S. or M.S. in CS, EE, CE, Telecommunications, or a related field
  • Strong foundation in linear algebra, probability, calculus, and optimization
  • Strong programming skills in Python; MATLAB/C++ is a plus
  • Background in machine learning; reinforcement learning/DRL is a strong plus
  • Knowledge of wireless communications, networking, or signal processing
  • Interest in working with UAVs, edge-AI platforms, simulation, and experimental systems
  • Strong research potential and ability to learn independently

How to Apply: Email mehdi.sookhak@tamucc.edu with the subject "Ph.D. Application – CIM Lab – Spring 2027", including (1) your CV and academic transcripts, (2) a brief description of your research interests and relevant experience, and (3) relevant publications, GitHub, or project links, if available.
